### What details should I have ready when I ask for help?
- **Order questions:** your order number. Verification may be required.
- **Product questions:** the product or model and your question.
- **Technical issues:** the model, the symptom, any exact error message, the device or version if relevant, and the steps you have already tried.
- **Returns:** the item, order number, delivery date, and its condition.
- **Billing:** the transaction date, amount, currency, and order reference. Never share a full card number.
- **Warranty:** the model, purchase date, the symptom, and proof of purchase through an approved channel.

### What should I include when I contact support?
Include your goal, your order or case reference, the product or model, a short summary of the issue, the dates and any error messages, and the steps you have already tried and the results. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, full payment details, or unnecessary sensitive data.

## Shipping

### How long does shipping take?
- Processing takes an estimated 1–2 business days.
- Standard delivery takes an estimated 3–7 business days after dispatch.
- Delivery estimates are not guarantees.

### Where can I track my order?
Use the order tracking page at https://northstarhome.example/track. Live order status is not available in this chat, so do not rely on it for tracking events or delivery dates.

### Why does my tracking show nothing yet?
Tracking may take up to 24 hours after dispatch to show its first carrier scan.
1. Confirm the dispatch date from your shipping email.
2. If less than 24 hours have passed, the first scan may still be pending.
3. If more than 24 hours have passed, check the tracking page and contact support.

A parcel is not considered lost unless the carrier or the support team confirms it.

### What if my order is late?
Compare the dispatch date and current tracking with the 3–7 business day delivery estimate. Estimates are not guarantees. If the estimate has passed, use the tracking page and contact support so a case can be created. A new delivery date can only be given once the carrier confirms an estimate.

### Tracking says delivered but I did not receive my parcel
1. Check the delivery location shown, with household members, building reception, or the parcel area.
2. Review the carrier's delivery notes or photo if available.
3. If the parcel is still missing, contact support with your order number and the delivery date.

A refund or replacement is not automatic for a missing parcel.

### Can I change my order or delivery address?
- Orders generally cannot be edited after warehouse processing begins.
- Before processing begins, an edit may be possible depending on the order system.
- After processing but before dispatch, editing may no longer be possible. Contact support.
- After dispatch, address corrections must be requested through the carrier's official correction process, if available.

### What if I received the wrong or damaged item?
Confirm the item you ordered and the item you received. Photos may be requested through an approved upload channel. Then follow the damage or incorrect-item process through support. If the item is a safety hazard, stop using it. Free replacement or free return shipping depends on policy confirmation.

## Returns, refunds and exchanges

### What is the return policy?
- Most unused, non-final-sale items may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- Items should include their parts and original packaging when practical.
- Hygiene-sensitive, customized, and final-sale items may be excluded, subject to applicable law.
- Return authorization is required before an item is shipped back.

### How do I return an item?
Request return authorization at https://northstarhome.example/returns or contact support for verified instructions. Have the item, order number, and delivery date ready. Do not ship an item back before you receive verified instructions. The return address is only provided through the returns portal or by support.

### Can I return an item after 30 days?
The standard return window is 30 calendar days from delivery. If the item has a defect or you have an exceptional circumstance, support can review the case. Exceptions are not guaranteed.

### Where is my refund?
- After an approved return is received and inspected, the company aims to issue the refund within 5–10 business days.
- Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to show the credit.
- Refunds normally go to the original payment method unless an approved exception applies.
- A refund is not confirmed until eligibility and status are verified. If the stated window has passed, contact billing support.

### Can I return a final-sale or excluded item?
Exclusions depend on the exact item listing and the current policy. If it is unclear whether your item is excluded, contact support so a person can review it.

### Can I exchange an item?
Whether exchanges are available depends on current policy and stock. Live stock information is not available in this chat. A return-and-reorder option is only offered if current policy supports it.

## Cancellations and price adjustments

### Can I cancel my order?
- Cancellation may be possible before warehouse processing begins.
- After dispatch, use the return process once the item is delivered, unless support confirms another option.
- A cancellation request is not complete until the order system confirms it.

### Do you offer price adjustments?
Price adjustments are not guaranteed. A displayed price difference alone does not qualify for a credit. Check the current promotion policy or contact support. A credit requires approval.

## Billing and payments

### What should I do if my payment failed?
1. Note the exact error message and the checkout stage where it appeared.
2. Check the payment method in the secure checkout.
3. Retry through the official checkout or use another supported payment method.
4. If it keeps failing, contact billing support.

Never share your full card number, security code, or banking credentials in chat.

### What if I see an unexpected charge?
Have the date, amount, currency, and order reference ready. If you do not recognize the charge, support will follow the dispute and security process. A charge is not labelled as fraud without evidence.

### What if I was charged twice?
Two entries can be two completed charges, or one completed charge and one pending authorization. A pending authorization is not a completed charge. How long a bank takes to release a pending authorization cannot be guaranteed. Contact billing support if the duplicate is confirmed or the result is unclear.

## Products and troubleshooting

### GlowDesk LED Lamp

#### What are the features of the GlowDesk LED Lamp?
It has adjustable brightness and colour temperature. The standard package includes a USB power adapter unless the order listing says otherwise. Wattage, control sequences, and compatibility details are only in the current product guide at https://northstarhome.example/guides.

#### What if my GlowDesk lamp will not turn on?
1. Check that the plug is securely connected.
2. If safe, test a known-working outlet.
3. Inspect the adapter and cable for visible damage without taking them apart.
4. Stop using it if it is unusually hot, smoking, emitting a burning smell, or visibly damaged.
5. If it still does not work, contact warranty support.

### BrewMate Kettle

#### What are the features of the BrewMate Kettle?
It is an electric kettle with automatic shutoff. Model-specific details and indicator meanings are in the product guide.

#### What if my BrewMate kettle will not turn on?
1. Confirm it is seated correctly on its base and that the outlet works.
2. Let it cool if it recently completed a heating cycle.
3. Check the model-specific guide for indicator meanings.
4. Do not open the electrical housing or bypass the automatic shutoff.
5. If it still fails, contact warranty support.

#### My kettle smells burnt, sparks, or leaks
Stop using it. Disconnect power only if it is safe, and do not touch wet electrical parts. Do not try it again. If there is active smoke, fire, or immediate danger, move away and contact local emergency services. Report the problem to product-safety support.

### PureAir Mini

#### What are the features of the PureAir Mini?
It is a compact room air purifier with a replaceable filter that is specified by model.

#### What if the airflow seems weak on my PureAir Mini?
1. Check that the intake and outlet are not blocked.
2. Confirm the correct filter is installed and check the replacement guidance.
3. Check the model-specific guide for fan-speed settings.
4. Stop using it and contact support if it overheats or smells burnt.

#### Does the PureAir Mini remove every virus or treat medical conditions?
Northstar Home does not claim that the PureAir Mini treats medical conditions, removes every pollutant or airborne virus, or guarantees a health outcome. See the verified product documentation for approved claims.

### StackSmart Storage Set

#### What if a StackSmart item does not fit my space?
Dimensions and materials vary by listing.
1. Identify the component and the space it should fit.
2. Check the current product listing for the verified dimensions.
3. Measure your available space, including drawer or door clearance.
4. If dimensions are not available, contact product support instead of estimating.

#### What if a StackSmart component is missing?
Have your order number ready and identify the missing part using the approved parts list. Replacement parts follow the replacement-parts process. A free replacement is not promised before eligibility is confirmed.

## Warranty, repairs and product safety

### What warranty do Northstar Home products have?
Eligible Northstar Home-branded small appliances have a 1-year limited warranty from the purchase date. Terms, exclusions, proof-of-purchase requirements, and regional rules apply. A final warranty determination requires checking the current policy and your purchase details.

### How do I make a warranty claim?
1. Have your product model, purchase date, region, and the symptom ready.
2. Provide proof of purchase through the approved channel only.
3. Support checks the current warranty policy and exclusions.
4. If eligibility is uncertain or disputed, an authorized agent reviews the case.

Repair, replacement, or coverage is not promised before verification.

### What should I do if a product smokes, sparks, leaks, or smells burnt?
1. Stop using it.
2. Disconnect power only if it is safe.
3. Do not keep testing it, open the device, or bypass a safety feature.
4. If there is active fire, smoke exposure, or immediate danger, move to safety and contact local emergency services.
5. Report the problem to product-safety support.

### How long do repairs take?
Repair time depends on the case status and the documented service target. If you have no status update, contact support. A repair completion date is not promised without a confirmed status.

## When a human agent will step in

### When will my issue be referred to a human agent?
An issue is referred to a person when:
- You ask for a human.
- You request a policy exception or discretionary compensation.
- Identity verification is required but cannot be completed in chat.
- You report a suspected account compromise, payment fraud, privacy incident, or product-safety issue.
